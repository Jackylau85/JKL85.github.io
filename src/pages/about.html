My Professional Background

International Logistics

Wire & Cable Products

8 years of experience

I have eight years of experience working with wire and cable products, including USB, M12, and audio-related applications.

My work has involved understanding product specifications, wire materials, gauge, shielding, and cost considerations. This background helps me approach connectivity requirements with a practical product and commercial perspective.

Vehicle Electronics

3 years of experience

My current focus is vehicle displays and monitoring products used in commercial vehicle and industrial applications.

I pay particular attention to how products are used in practice: what the operator needs to see, which video inputs and connections are required, how the equipment is installed, and what conditions it must withstand.

I believe that understanding the application is an important starting point for discussing the right product.

02 · WORKING APPROACH

How I Approach My Work

I work from the customer's requirements rather than starting with a product catalogue.

Before discussing a solution, it is important to understand the application, operating environment, interfaces, installation constraints, and commercial expectations.

My role is to communicate product capabilities clearly, help clarify requirements, and coordinate discussions around suitable options. When a project involves detailed engineering questions, those questions should be addressed with the appropriate technical team.

I value straightforward communication, realistic expectations, and practical solutions over complicated presentations or claims that cannot be supported.

03 · PURPOSE

Why I Built This Website

This website is a place to share practical knowledge and observations from my work.

The topics include vehicle displays, commercial vehicle rear-view systems, cameras, wire and cable products, product selection, installation considerations, and common problems encountered in real applications.

Some articles focus on questions that appear simple but can be difficult to diagnose in practice — for example, why a monitor intermittently shows “No Signal,” or what to check before replacing a camera or cable.

My goal is to make these topics easier to understand and to encourage more informed discussions between customers, suppliers, and product teams.

I will continue adding insights as I learn from products, applications, and business conversations.
