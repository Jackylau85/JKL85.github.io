Why Does a Vehicle Monitor Sometimes Show “No Signal”?

A practical guide to common causes of intermittent video loss in commercial vehicle rear-view systems.

Category: Monitors

Publication date: 2026-10-20

Introduction

When a vehicle monitor intermittently shows “No Signal,” the problem may come from the camera, power supply, cable, connector, or video signal compatibility. The monitor itself is not necessarily faulty.

1. Check the camera power supply

Check whether the camera receives stable power while the problem occurs. On a working vehicle, vibration or an intermittent electrical connection may cause the camera to restart or stop transmitting video.

2. Inspect cables and connectors

Inspect connectors for looseness, corrosion, moisture, damaged pins, and cable damage. Pay particular attention to connection points exposed to vibration, water, and repeated movement.

3. Confirm video compatibility

Confirm that the camera output format is supported by the monitor input. A mismatch between supported video formats can cause a missing or unstable picture.

Conclusion

Check the system step by step before replacing components. Recording when the fault occurs and checking power, cables, connectors, and signal compatibility can help narrow down the cause.
